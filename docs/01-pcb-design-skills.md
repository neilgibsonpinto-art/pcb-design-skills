# PCB Design Skills

## Core Technical Skills
- **Electronics fundamentals**: Ohm's law, analog and digital circuits, power supplies, and signal behavior.
- **Schematic capture**: Clear, hierarchical schematics with correct net naming and annotation.
- **Component selection**: Reading datasheets, choosing footprints, and assessing availability, lifecycle, and cost.
- **Footprint and library management**: Accurate land patterns that follow IPC standards.
- **Board layout**: Placement, routing, layer stack-up, board outline, and mechanical fit.
- **Design rules (DRC/ERC)**: Trace width, clearance, via sizes, and annular rings matched to fab capabilities.

## Advanced Skills
- **Signal integrity**: Impedance control, differential pairs, length matching, and return paths.
- **Power integrity**: Decoupling, power planes, current capacity, and IR drop.
- **High-speed design**: DDR, USB, PCIe, and Ethernet routing.
- **EMI/EMC**: Grounding, shielding, filtering, and minimizing loop areas.
- **RF and microwave layout**: Controlled impedance lines, antenna keep-outs, and matching networks.
- **Thermal management**: Thermal vias, copper pours, heat sinks, and spacing.
- **Mixed-signal design**: Separating analog and digital, and partitioning grounds.

## Manufacturing Knowledge
- **DFM/DFA/DFT**: Design for manufacturability, assembly, and testability.
- **Fabrication**: Layer counts, HDI, microvias, flex and rigid-flex, and surface finishes (HASL, ENIG).
- **Outputs**: Gerbers, drill files, BOM, pick-and-place files, and assembly drawings.
- **Standards**: IPC-2221, IPC-7351, IPC-A-600, and IPC-6012.

## Tools
- **EDA**: KiCad, Altium Designer, Autodesk Fusion/Eagle, OrCAD/Allegro, and EasyEDA.
- **Simulation**: LTspice, ngspice, and HyperLynx.
- **Version control**: Git for design files.

## Practical and Soft Skills
- Prototyping, soldering, and debugging with an oscilloscope and multimeter.
- Documentation and revision control.
- Collaboration with firmware, mechanical, and manufacturing teams.
- Attention to detail and project management.
