# Resources

> Verify links and editions before relying on them. Some items are paid.

## Books
- *The Art of Electronics*, Horowitz and Hill
- *Right the First Time: A Practical Handbook on High-Speed PCB and System Design*, Lee Ritchey
- *High-Speed Digital Design: A Handbook of Black Magic*, Howard Johnson and Martin Graham
- *Signal and Power Integrity – Simplified*, Eric Bogatin
- *Printed Circuit Board Designer's Reference: Basics*, Christopher Robertson
- *EMC and the Printed Circuit Board*, Mark Montrose
- *Fundamentals of Power Electronics*, Erickson and Maksimovic
- *RF Circuit Design*, Chris Bowick
- *Small Unmanned Aircraft: Theory and Practice*, Beard and McLain

## Free Courses and Tutorials
- [KiCad Documentation](https://docs.kicad.org/)
- [Contextual Electronics](https://contextualelectronics.com/) (KiCad-based; some paid content)
- [SparkFun Learn](https://learn.sparkfun.com/)
- [Adafruit Learn](https://learn.adafruit.com/)
- [Phil's Lab (YouTube)](https://www.youtube.com/@PhilsLab): STM32, KiCad, and flight-controller-style designs
- [Robert Feranec (YouTube)](https://www.youtube.com/@RobertFeranec): PCB design walkthroughs
- [Altium Academy (YouTube)](https://www.youtube.com/@AltiumAcademy)
- [EEVblog (YouTube)](https://www.youtube.com/@EEVblog)
- [Analog Devices Education](https://www.analog.com/en/education.html)
- [Texas Instruments Training](https://www.ti.com/video/series/ti-precision-labs.html)

## Application Notes and Guides
- TI: Layout guidelines for switching power supplies (search "TI SNVA021")
- Analog Devices: *A Practical Guide to High-Speed PCB Layout*
- Henry Ott: EMC design resources
- ST: AN4488 (STM32 hardware development) and AN4891 (PCB layout)

## Tools
- [KiCad](https://www.kicad.org/): free and open source
- [LTspice](https://www.analog.com/en/resources/design-tools-and-calculators/ltspice-simulator.html)
- [Saturn PCB Toolkit](https://saturnpcb.com/saturn-pcb-design-toolkit/): trace width and impedance
- [JLCPCB](https://jlcpcb.com/) and [PCBWay](https://www.pcbway.com/): fabrication and assembly
- [LCSC](https://www.lcsc.com/), [Digi-Key](https://www.digikey.com/), and [Mouser](https://www.mouser.com/): components
- [SnapEDA](https://www.snapeda.com/) and [Ultra Librarian](https://www.ultralibrarian.com/): symbols and footprints

## Standards
- IPC: [ipc.org](https://www.ipc.org/) (IPC-2221, IPC-7351, IPC-A-600, IPC-6012, IPC-A-610)
- RTCA: [rtca.org](https://www.rtca.org/) (DO-254, DO-160, DO-178C)
- SAE: [sae.org](https://www.sae.org/) (ARP4754A, ARP4761)
- MIL-STD-810 and MIL-STD-461 (US DoD)
